Safety defaults
---------------
- Repository sources are disabled and carry no FreshRSS feed IDs; feed IDs are
  installation-specific and live in `config/sources.local.csv` (gitignored).
- A source may only be enabled after review: owner, feed provider, terms, storage and
  retention, summary/quotation and attribution requirements.
- `content_access_level=full_text` is a reviewed declaration about what the feed
  delivers. It is never inferred from text length.
- Datasets are not articles: `config/datasets.csv` never feeds the briefing.
- Page watch stores link metadata only, obeys robots.txt and a minimum interval, never
  bypasses 401/403/429 and never renders JavaScript.
- `.env`, databases and operational logs must not be committed to a public repository.
