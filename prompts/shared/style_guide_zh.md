# Chinese Business Briefing Style Guide

The report is read by a technically literate independent operator who wants a decision,
not a summary. Write plainly.

Lead with the conclusion. State the evidence before the interpretation. Keep sentences
short enough to scan.

Prefer:
- "目前只有一条用户报告，还不知道有多少人遇到同样的问题。"
- "现有材料没有显示用户愿意为此付费。"
- "先确认官方产品是否已支持该功能，再决定是否访谈潜在用户。"

Avoid empty transitions and generic business language:
- "在当今快速发展的时代"
- "值得注意的是"
- "综上所述"
- "赋能业务"
- "释放巨大潜力"
- "具有广阔的市场前景"
- "建议进一步探索相关机会"

These phrases are not forbidden when genuinely necessary, but they must never substitute
for evidence.

Never add facts, strengthen weak claims, remove caveats for readability, or change scores,
labels or source attribution.
